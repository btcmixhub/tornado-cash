# tornado-cash
Tornado Cash Private Cryptocurrency Mixing

[Tornado Cash](https://tornadocashmixer.com/) is a decentralized, non-custodial crypto mixer built on Ethereum. Protect your on-chain privacy by breaking the traceable link between deposit and withdrawal addresses.

[Learn How It Works](https://tornadocashmixer.com/)

How the [Tornado Cash Mixer](https://tornadocashmixer.com/) Works

The TornadoCash protocol leverages zero-knowledge proofs to sever the on-chain connection between a source and destination wallet. Every step is handled by immutable smart contracts — no intermediary ever touches your funds.

1) Deposit Crypto
   
Send a fixed denomination of ETH, BNB, or USDT into the Tornado Cash smart contract. The contract generates a unique cryptographic note that serves as your withdrawal key.

2) Wait & Blend
   
Your deposit enters the anonymity pool alongside others. The longer assets remain in the crypto mixer pool, the larger the anonymity set and the stronger the privacy guarantee.

3) Withdraw Privately
   
Present your cryptographic note to withdraw funds to any wallet. A zk-SNARK proof confirms you have a valid deposit without revealing which one — completing the mixing process.
